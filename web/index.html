A better use of your spare fifteen minutes

LUMIO is built around one idea: small, steady reading beats endless scrolling.

  • Real sources

    Lessons are built from classic books such as Darwin, Einstein, Thucydides and William James, and show where each idea came from.

  • About fifteen minutes

    One lesson at a time, ending with three to five takeaways. Your place is saved as you read.

  • A gentle streak

    Build a daily habit without pressure. No quizzes, no tests, and no guilt-driven notifications.

How it works

You never have to pick a book, a chapter or a course.

  1. Pick what you care about

    Choose a few subjects when you start. You can change them any time.

  2. Read today’s lesson

    LUMIO picks the next lesson for you and keeps a few more ready in your queue.

  3. Keep going

    Finish, see your takeaways, and come back tomorrow. Your streak and progress stay with your account.

Install on Android

It takes about a minute.

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser, because LUMIO is not on the Play Store yet.
  3. Open LUMIO and create an account with your email or Google.

Once installed, LUMIO tells you inside the app when a new version is ready.

Questions

What is LUMIO?

LUMIO is a free Android app that gives you one focused, 15-minute reading lesson a day, chosen for you from classic public-domain books. Each lesson ends with three to five takeaways and lists its sources.

Is LUMIO free?

Yes. Download it and read as many lessons as you like.

Is it available on iPhone?

Not yet. LUMIO is Android-first, and iPhone is planned for later.

Why isn’t it on the Play Store?

LUMIO is in early release, so it is shared as a direct download for now. Android may ask you to allow the install once.

Where do the lessons come from?

From a library of public-domain books and openly licensed articles. Each lesson is written from approved passages and lists its sources.

Will it nag me to keep my streak?

No. Streaks are there to encourage you, never to guilt you. There are no quizzes and no tests.
