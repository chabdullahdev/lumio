What you get from fifteen minutes

Scrolling leaves nothing behind. LUMIO is built so that every session leaves you with something you keep.

  • A notebook that fills itself

    Finish a lesson and its key takeaways are saved for you. Search them any time: a growing collection of ideas from Darwin, Einstein, Thucydides, William James and more.

  • Nothing to choose

    No hunting for books or building a reading list. LUMIO picks the next lesson from the great books, matched to your interests, and keeps a few ready.

  • Real sources, no nagging

    Every lesson shows where its ideas came from. A gentle streak builds the habit, with no quizzes, no tests and no guilt-driven notifications.

How it works

You never have to pick a book, a chapter or a course.

  1. Pick what you care about

    Choose a few subjects when you start. You can change them any time.

  2. Read today’s lesson

    LUMIO picks the next lesson for you and keeps a few more ready in your queue.

  3. Keep going

    Finish, see your takeaways, and they join your notebook. Come back tomorrow; your streak and progress stay with your account.

Install it like an app

No app store, no download file. It takes about ten seconds.

  1. Android or computer (Chrome, Edge): open LUMIO, then tap Install when it appears, or use the browser menu and choose Install app.
  2. iPhone or iPad (Safari): open LUMIO, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open LUMIO from your home screen and create an account with your email or Google.

Questions

What is LUMIO?

LUMIO is a free learning app that gives you one focused, 15-minute lesson a day, chosen for you from classic public-domain books. Each lesson ends with three to five takeaways, which are saved in your own notebook, and lists its sources.

Is LUMIO free?

Yes. Open it and read as many lessons as you like.

How do I install it?

Open LUMIO in your browser and tap Install, or on iPhone use Share, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen like any app. There is no app store and no download file.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. LUMIO works in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, Windows and Mac, and installs to your home screen on all of them.

Where do the lessons come from?

From a library of public-domain books and openly licensed articles. Each lesson is written from approved passages and lists its sources.

What is the notebook?

Every time you finish a lesson, its key takeaways are saved to your notebook automatically. You can search it any time to revisit the ideas you have kept.

Will it nag me to keep my streak?

No. Streaks are there to encourage you, never to guilt you. There are no quizzes and no tests.
