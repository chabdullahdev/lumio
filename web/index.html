Learn something real, every day.

LUMIO is a daily lesson app. Each day you get one focused, fifteen-minute reading lesson, chosen for you from great books, instead of another hour of scrolling.

Download APK

Android app · free · about 29 MB

How it works

  1. Pick what you care about

    Choose a few subjects. You never have to pick a book, a chapter or a course.

  2. Read one lesson a day

    About fifteen minutes, built from real sources, ending with three to five takeaways.

  3. Build a gentle streak

    Your progress is saved as you read. Miss a day and nothing bad happens, no guilt, no nagging.

What you can learn

  • Geopolitics
  • History
  • Islam
  • Politics
  • Psychology
  • Science

Lessons draw on classic books in the public domain, such as Darwin, Einstein, Thucydides, William James and Mill, and always show where an idea came from. No quizzes, no tests, just understanding.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download APK on your phone.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser, because LUMIO is not on the Play Store yet.
  3. Open LUMIO and tap Continue with Google, or use your email.

Once installed, LUMIO tells you inside the app when a new version is ready.
